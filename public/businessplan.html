Section 1

1. Executive summary

Vireo is a web tool that turns long podcast and interview videos into short, ready-to-post clips for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. It also gives video editors files they can keep editing. We are a two-founder company based in Morocco, selling online to podcasters worldwide.

$15,500funding needed, maximum, in 3 steps
Jun 27first profitable month (base case)
$179,517revenue in 2028 (base case)
72%gross margin after AI, cloud and payment fees

The problem

Turning one podcast episode into 5–10 good short clips takes an editor 3–5 hours. Freelancers charge $25–75 per clip. AI tools exist, but their clips often need fixing, and editors can't keep editing them.

Our answer

Clips that need less fixing (great captions, no "ums", speaker in frame), a quality checklist on every clip, and an easy hand-off to editing software. Only 2 of 41 competing tools offer that hand-off; none checks clip quality.

Customers

Weekly video podcasts and the freelance editors and small agencies who make their clips. Plans at $19, $49 and $149 per month.

Money

It costs us about $0.62 to turn a 1-hour episode into 10 clips. Expected revenue: $42,994 in year 1, $179,517 in 2028, $304,815 in Jan–Oct 2029 (base case).

3. The problem

Podcasts moved to video. To grow, every show must post short clips on TikTok, Reels and Shorts, every week. Making those clips is slow, repetitive work.

≈ 230 minutes per episode. Estimate, to be measured with real editors in pilots.

Why existing AI tools are not enough

  • Clips need fixing. Users report clips that start mid-sentence, captions hidden under the TikTok buttons, or the wrong person in frame.
  • No hand-off. Most tools only give a finished MP4. To change one word, an editor starts over. Only 2 of 41 tools export to editing software.
  • Billing frustration. 20% of the market leader's Trustpilot reviews are 1-star, mostly about credits and cancelling. Klap: 31%.

5. Market

The market is large, growing, and already proven by a leader making about $10 million a year. Basic clipping is crowded; quality and editor hand-off are not.

58%of Americans 12+ consume podcasts monthly (167M people), an all-time high (Edison 2026)
37–44%of weekly US podcast fans use YouTube most, ahead of Spotify and Apple
1–1.5MYouTube channels publishing video podcasts (estimate)
~$10M2025 revenue of OpusClip, the leader (estimate); valued ~$215M in 2025

AI video editing tools market (one estimate)

$1.6B → $9.3B (+42% per year). Another firm sizes the broader AI video generation + editing market at $3.67B in 2026. Definitions differ, so we use these only as direction.

Our reachable market (bottom-up)

What this means

If 200,000 regular video podcasts each paid an average $33/month, the reachable market would be about $79.2 million per year. Our base case needs only 928 customers by October 2029, under 0.5% of that group, to reach $37,130/month. Agencies and freelance editors add to this.

Trends working for us

  • Podcasts became video. 57% of podcast fans both listen and watch; only 21% are audio-only (Edison 2026). Spotify has nearly 500,000 video podcasts.
  • Young audiences watch. 84% of Gen Z podcast listeners consume podcasts with video.
  • Short clips are how shows grow. TikTok, Reels and Shorts are the main way new listeners discover a podcast, so every show needs clips every week.
  • AI costs keep falling. Speech-to-text now costs about $0.21 per hour of audio, and small AI models cost cents per episode. That makes a 70%+ margin realistic.
  • Basic clipping is commoditized. Free tools exist, which pushes paying customers toward quality and workflow: our focus.

8. Business model and pricing

We sell monthly subscriptions. The price unit is simple: minutes of finished clips. Ten 45-second clips = 7.5 clip-minutes. Analyzing long episodes is generous because it costs us little.

350 clip-minutes (~470 clips), 100 h, client spaces & approvals

Expected revenue mix

Creator 40% of revenue (70% of customers)Studio 37% (25%)Agency 23% (5%)

Extra revenue

  • Extra clip-minutes: $0.35 each (customer opts in)
  • AI voice in other languages: $1.50 per clip per language (costs us ~$0.30)
  • Archive pack: analyze 5,000 old minutes for $49
  • Yearly plans: 2 months free (improves cash and lowers churn)

Why these prices

$19 avoids a direct "$15 vs $15" comparison with OpusClip while giving 4× more video analysis. Average revenue per customer is assumed to grow from $33 (2027) to $36 (2028) and $40 (2029) as Studio and Agency plans grow. Prices are tested with pilot customers before being fixed.

Why SARL

With a payment company (Merchant of Record), all our revenue comes from one "client": the payment company. Under auto-entrepreneur rules, income above 80,000 MAD from one client is taxed at 30%, and total service income is capped at 200,000 MAD (≈ $21,277). Our base case passes that in 2027. A SARL avoids both limits. Confirm with a Moroccan chartered accountant.

Getting paid

Stripe does not serve Moroccan businesses directly. Lemon Squeezy (and Polar) sell for us, handle sales tax in every country, and pay out to Moroccan bank accounts. Fees: 5% + $0.50, +1.5% international, +0.5% subscriptions, 1% payout fee.

Tax and exchange rules

Services sold abroad are generally outside Moroccan VAT; invoices say so. Foreign income must be reported to the Office des Changes. Company profit is taxed at 20% (IS) below 100M MAD of profit. All to confirm with an accountant.

Video rights

We accept only uploads, podcast feeds and Drive/Dropbox links. We do not download YouTube videos: YouTube's developer rules forbid it without written approval. Users confirm they own the content.

Privacy and licences

Written privacy policy before the first pilot; no AI training on customer videos; deletion on request. Remotion (our rendering library) is free for teams up to 3 people; from 4 people we pay its licence ($100/month minimum + $0.01 per render), included in the model.

Section 20

20. Long-term vision

Our goal is a profitable, independent company first. Bigger options open only if the numbers work.

1. Profitable small company

Base case: $445,560 yearly revenue run-rate by late 2029, team of 5, profitable. Founders paid from revenue.

2. Raise money to grow faster

If growth looks like the strong case, outside investors could fund more marketing and hires. That decision comes after real traction, not before.

3. Be acquired

Clipping companies attract buyers: OpusClip was reportedly valued around $215M on ~$10M revenue (2025). A podcast host, editing-software company or social tool could buy a focused, profitable player.

Where the product goes later

  • More languages (French, Arabic, Spanish captions and AI voices)
  • Learning from which clips perform best on each channel
  • Plugins inside Premiere and Resolve
  • Tools for brands running paid clipping campaigns
